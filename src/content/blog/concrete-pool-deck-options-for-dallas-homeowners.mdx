## Choosing the Right Concrete Pool Deck in Dallas

Your pool deck takes more punishment than almost any other concrete surface on your property. It bakes in the Dallas sun for months, gets splashed with chlorinated water daily, and needs to be safe to walk on with bare, wet feet. Choosing the right concrete finish makes the difference between a deck that performs for years and one that becomes a problem.

### Option 1: Spray-Texture Cool Deck

Cool deck is the most popular pool deck finish in Dallas for good reason. A textured acrylic coating is sprayed onto the concrete surface, creating a slightly rough texture that stays cooler underfoot than standard concrete. The light color reflects more heat, and the texture provides reliable grip when wet.

**Best for:** Homeowners who prioritize comfort and safety in the Texas heat. Cool deck surfaces can be 30–40% cooler than standard concrete on a hot day.

### Option 2: Stamped Concrete

Stamped concrete around a pool creates the look of natural stone, flagstone, or slate at a fraction of the cost. Patterns are pressed into freshly poured concrete, and color is added through integral pigments or surface-applied hardeners.

**Best for:** Homeowners who want a premium, custom appearance. Non-slip additives can be mixed into the sealer to maintain wet traction.

### Option 3: Broom Finish

A traditional broom-swept finish creates parallel texture lines that provide basic slip resistance. It is the most affordable pool deck option and works well for straightforward rectangular pools.

**Best for:** Budget-conscious projects where function matters more than decorative appeal.

### Option 4: Concrete Overlay and Resurfacing

If your existing pool deck is structurally sound but worn, stained, or outdated, a concrete overlay can transform it without full demolition. A thin layer of polymer-modified concrete is applied over the existing surface and can be stamped, textured, or spray-coated.

**Best for:** Renovations where the existing slab is still level and intact but needs a fresh surface.

### Key Considerations for Dallas Pool Decks

**Heat management.** Light-colored finishes and textured surfaces stay cooler. Dark colors and smooth finishes absorb and radiate more heat.

**Drainage.** The deck must slope away from the pool and away from your home's foundation. Proper grading prevents standing water and directs splash-out toward landscape drains.

**Chemical resistance.** Chlorine and salt splash will degrade unprotected concrete over time. Quality sealers protect the surface from chemical damage and UV fading.

**Expansion joints.** Pool decks need properly placed joints to handle the constant expansion and contraction cycle from Dallas temperature swings. Poorly jointed decks crack.

### What to Ask Your Contractor

1. What finish do you recommend for my pool layout and sun exposure?
2. How do you handle drainage around the pool equipment pad?
3. What sealer do you use, and how often does it need reapplication?
4. Do you add non-slip additives to the topcoat?
