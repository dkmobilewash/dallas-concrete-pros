## When Should You Pour Concrete in Dallas?

Timing matters when it comes to concrete work. Temperature, humidity, and weather conditions all affect how concrete cures, which directly impacts its strength and durability. In Dallas, the climate creates both ideal windows and challenging periods for concrete projects.

### Spring: The Sweet Spot (March–May)

Spring is the most popular season for concrete work in Dallas. Temperatures are moderate — typically 60–85 degrees — which is ideal for curing. Concrete needs to stay above 50 degrees for the first 48 hours to develop proper strength, and below 90 degrees to prevent surface cracking from rapid moisture loss.

The main risk in spring is rain. Dallas gets some of its heaviest rainfall in April and May. A surprise downpour on fresh concrete can damage the surface finish. Your contractor should monitor the forecast closely and have a rain plan.

### Fall: The Runner-Up (September–November)

Fall offers similar temperature advantages to spring with generally less rainfall. October and November are excellent months for concrete pours in Dallas. Temperatures are dropping from summer highs, and the moderate weather allows for controlled, even curing.

### Summer: Workable with Precautions (June–August)

Dallas summers regularly hit 100+ degrees with intense sun. Concrete can still be poured successfully in summer, but it requires extra attention. Contractors may start pours at dawn to avoid peak heat, use evaporation retarders, and apply curing compounds immediately after finishing. Hot-weather concrete needs more water for mixing and faster finishing because it sets up quickly.

### Winter: Proceed with Caution (December–February)

Dallas winters are mild compared to northern states, but overnight lows can dip below freezing. Concrete that freezes before it reaches adequate strength can be permanently damaged. Most concrete work can proceed during Dallas winters with proper planning — insulated blankets and heated enclosures protect fresh concrete during cold snaps.

### Scheduling Tips

1. **Book early for spring and fall** — these are peak seasons, and the best contractors fill up fast.
2. **Watch the 10-day forecast** — your contractor should be willing to reschedule if rain or extreme temperatures are expected.
3. **Plan for curing time** — concrete reaches functional strength in about 7 days and full strength in 28 days, regardless of season.
4. **Ask about mix adjustments** — experienced contractors adjust the concrete mix design based on conditions.
