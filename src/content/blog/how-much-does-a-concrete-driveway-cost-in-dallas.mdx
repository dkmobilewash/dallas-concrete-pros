## What Affects Concrete Driveway Costs in Dallas?

If you're planning a new driveway or replacing an existing one, cost is probably your first question. The truth is that concrete driveway pricing varies based on several factors specific to your project and property.

### Key Pricing Factors

**Size and thickness** are the biggest cost drivers. A standard two-car driveway is around 400–600 square feet. Most residential driveways are poured at 4 inches thick, though heavier vehicles may require 5–6 inches.

**Site preparation** can significantly affect cost. If your existing driveway needs to be demolished and hauled away, or if grading work is needed, that adds to the project total.

**Finish type** matters too. A basic broom finish is the most affordable option. Stamped concrete, exposed aggregate, or colored concrete all add cost but can dramatically improve curb appeal.

**Access and logistics** play a role. If the concrete truck can park close to the pour site, costs stay lower. Difficult access may require pumping, which adds to the total.

### Tips for Getting the Best Value

1. **Get multiple quotes** — but compare apples to apples. Make sure each quote includes the same thickness, finish, and site prep work.
2. **Don't choose on price alone** — a cheaper pour that cracks in two years costs more in the long run.
3. **Ask about reinforcement** — rebar or wire mesh adds cost upfront but prevents cracking and extends the life of your driveway.
4. **Time your project** — spring and fall are ideal for concrete work in Dallas, and contractors may have more availability during slower months.
